Brachistochrone (Cycloid) — interactive

Drag the start and end points. The brachistochrone is the fastest descent under uniform gravity (no friction) and is a cycloid segment.
Units / world scale
Tip: choose meters/grid and px/grid to match a physical scale you want. Travel times scale with √(length / g).
Visibility
Simulation
The bead starts from rest at the start point and slides without friction. Speed is from energy: v = √(2 g Δy).

Valid when the end is to the right and below the start (Δx > 0, Δy > 0). If not, the classical brachistochrone doesn’t apply (no purely downhill path).
Gestures
Drag points to move them. Drag empty space to pan. Pinch / Ctrl+wheel to zoom. Double-click / double-tap to reset view.
