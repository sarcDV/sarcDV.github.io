Birth of AI

1956

The Dartmouth Conference coins the term "Artificial Intelligence" and establishes AI as a formal field of study.

Organized by John McCarthy, Marvin Minsky, Nathaniel Rochester, and Claude Shannon, the conference laid the foundation for decades of AI research.

Category: Foundation
