How MAIAT Works

Automating the complex world of malware analysis — so you can focus on what matters.

  • 🤖
    Fully Automated Analysis

    MAIAT automates file ingestion, sandboxing, and behavioral analysis, reducing manual effort by up to 40%.

  • ⚡
    Rapid & Accurate Intelligence

    Extracts Indicators of Compromise (IoCs), maps threats to MITRE ATT&CK, and generates structured reports for your SIEM — improving detection accuracy by 25%.

  • 🔓
    Democratized Expertise

    Delivers enterprise-grade malware analysis at an accessible cost, making advanced cyber defense available to everyone.

  • 🚀
    Accelerated Response

    Reduces Mean Time to Detect and Respond (MTTD/MTTR) by providing immediate, explainable findings for faster containment.

MAIAT turns the endless stream of threats into a structured flow of intelligence, empowering your team to respond faster and smarter.

The Automated Analysis Engine

  • 📥
    INGESTION & TRIAGE

    Samples can be uploaded directly or retrieved from a designated feed. The system automatically classifies and prioritizes samples for further analysis.

  • 🔍
    STATIC & DYNAMIC ANALYSIS

    Static analysis involves disassembly, string extraction, and entropy analysis. Dynamic analysis detonates samples in a secure sandbox, logging all activities.

  • 🧠
    BEHAVIORAL ANALYSIS & INTELLIGENCE EXTRACTION

    ML and NLP engines analyze sandbox logs to interpret malicious intent, extracting key IoCs and a detailed behavioral timeline.

  • 📊
    ATTRIBUTION & REPORTING

    Actions are mapped to the MITRE ATT&CK framework, and structured reports are auto-generated in formats like STIX, JSON, or Markdown for SIEM/CTI platforms.
