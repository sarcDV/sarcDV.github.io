How Our Technology Works

Our multispectral imaging acquisitions are the cornerstone of our technology. This advanced process captures spectral reflectance at the pixel level, enabling us to estimate melanin levels, hemoglobin concentration, and hydration. This data is essential for effectively mapping pigmentation irregularities and vascularity, allowing us to address specific skin concerns.

The Problem & Our Solution

Why traditional skincare fails and how we're different

The Generic Skincare Problem

Traditional skincare products are designed for the "average" person, but there's no such thing as average skin. Your skin is influenced by your unique genetics, environment, lifestyle, and health conditions.

This one-size-fits-all approach often leads to ineffective results, wasted money, and sometimes even skin irritation or damage.

Simphonie: Our AI-Powered Personalization

We use advanced AI algorithms that analyze hundreds of data points about your skin, including texture, hydration levels, pigmentation, and environmental factors.

Our system creates truly personalized formulations that adapt to your skin's changing needs, ensuring optimal results every time.

Opera: Real-Time Adaptation Technology

Unlike static products, our system continuously learns and adapts. As your environment changes, your lifestyle evolves, or your skin responds to treatment, we adjust your formulations accordingly.

This ensures you always receive the most effective skincare for your current needs.

A Robotic system fully integrated with our software ecosystem Simphonie that is composed by an input module and a robotic module for automatic skincare product preparation.

Adagio: Mobile App

Our mobile app provides a seamless experience, allowing you to manage your skincare routine on the go.

Receive personalized product recommendations, track your skin's progress, and get real-time updates on your formulations.
