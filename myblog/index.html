Welcome to My Blog

Exploring science, networks, dynamics, and computation — one paper at a time.

This blog is a personal space where I dive deep into foundational scientific papers, unpack their mathematics, simulate their models, and reflect on their implications. Everything here is static, serverless, and open — built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and free to access.

I begin with a classic: “Collective dynamics of ‘small-world’ networks” by Duncan J. Watts and Steven H. Strogatz (Nature, 1998) — a landmark study that revealed how real-world networks balance local clustering with global connectivity.

Feel free to explore the dedicated article, which includes rigorous definitions, interactive simulations, and real-world context.
